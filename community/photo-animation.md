# How to check a portrait animation from a single reference photo


Pick a photo you have permission to animate. In FreeVideo AI, Image to Video takes one reference. Use PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 10 MB. For a first attempt, keep the camera still and ask for one small movement.

The portrait example in our guide asks for a gentle blink and a smile. In the sampled frames, the subject’s eyes close and reopen and her head angle changes slightly. Those observations leave an important question open. What happens between the sampled frames?

Play the clip at normal speed, then pause at the beginning, middle and end. Inspect the eyes, mouth and hair against the source. If several features change at once, simplify the movement for the next attempt. For a product photo, apply the same check to its silhouette and any lettering.

The [original guide with the recorded example](https://freevideoai.app/image-to-video) includes its prompt, observed result and limitations.
