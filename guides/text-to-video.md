# Text to Video with FreeVideo AI: Write Better Single-Shot Prompts

Use Text to Video when you have a scene idea without a starting image. FreeVideo AI's H3 Standard workflow uses MiniMax H3 to create a short MP4 from your description at 6 credits per task. Cloud quotes vary by model, resolution and duration and require eligible purchased credits.

[Open Text to Video](https://freevideoai.app/ai-video-generator?mode=text-to-video)

## A prompt structure you can reuse

Describe the scene in this order: **subject → action → camera → setting and light → continuity**. Specific visual instructions are easier to assess than a string of quality adjectives.

| Part | Useful instruction | Why it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | A clear glass perfume bottle on a dark stone plinth | Establishes one main object |
| Action | A small amount of mist drifts behind the bottle | Gives the scene one movement |
| Camera | A slow, straight push-in | Avoids competing camera directions |
| Light | Soft side light with warm rim highlights | Describes the intended appearance |
| Continuity | One continuous shot; the bottle remains upright | States the desired scene behavior |

These are guidance, not hard constraints. The model can still change shape, text or motion.

## Generate a first version

1. Sign in and select Text to Video.
2. Choose **5 seconds** for a short concept test.
3. Pick **16:9** for a landscape composition or **9:16** for portrait.
4. Write a single scene. Put the most important subject and action first.
5. Generate, wait for the final task status, then preview and download.
6. Compare the result with your requested action before revising.

For portrait output, describe where the subject sits in the tall frame. Avoid a wide group shot if the important details need to remain visible on a phone.

## Example: a product mood clip

```text
A clear glass perfume bottle with a plain, unbranded label stands upright on a dark stone plinth. A thin veil of mist drifts slowly behind it. The camera gently pushes straight toward the bottle. Soft side light and a warm rim highlight reveal the glass. The bottle stays centered in one continuous shot.
```

Use this for an imagined product concept. If a real product's shape or packaging must be preserved, start with a photo in [Image to Video](https://freevideoai.app/ai-video-generator?mode=image-to-video) instead; image guidance still does not guarantee exact identity.

## Example: a landscape establishing shot

```text
A quiet lakeside cabin at sunrise, reflected in still water. A few low clouds drift across the distant hills. The camera slowly moves forward over the water toward the cabin. Natural golden light, calm movement, one continuous wide shot.
```

## Revise one problem at a time

| Observed problem | Try this change |
| --- | --- |
| The camera swings around unexpectedly | Replace several camera instructions with “locked camera” or one slow push-in |
| Too many things move | Keep one moving subject and describe the background as still |
| The scene changes halfway through | Remove cuts, location changes and multiple actions |
| The product label becomes distorted | Avoid requiring readable text; add accurate typography in an editor |
| Portrait framing cuts off the subject | Ask for the complete subject centered with space above and below |

Do not assume adding “8K” or “cinematic” enables a new output resolution. FreeVideo AI exposes duration and aspect ratio controls; output resolution follows the workflow.

## When to use 10 or 15 seconds

Choose a longer duration when a simple movement needs more time, such as a gentle reveal. Keep the action focused. If your idea needs three scenes, generate separate clips and assemble them in an editor rather than packing three shots into one prompt.

## What this workflow does not do

H3 Standard exports a silent MP4 if gifted credits fund any part of the task. Standard tasks funded entirely with eligible purchased credits can retain native audio. Exact brand lettering, stable small details and complex physical interactions can be unreliable. Inspect the downloaded result before using it in a product listing or campaign.
