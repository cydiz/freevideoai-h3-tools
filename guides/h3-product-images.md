# Plan a Product Image in H3 Image Studio

Sign in to [FreeVideo AI](https://freevideoai.app/ai-image-generator) to generate an image for 3 credits. You can prepare a product concept offline or animate a photograph you already have in Video Studio.

[Open H3 Image Studio](https://freevideoai.app/ai-image-generator)

## Plan the composition

Use a text prompt to describe a still-image concept, including the product shape, materials, lighting, background and framing. Check an offline plan with `node bin/cli.js validate image examples/image.json` from the repository root. The example is a configuration fixture for the validator.

A rendered concept can differ from a real product's cap, proportions or label. For a listing that must show the exact item, use approved product photography and an editing workflow that preserves it.

For a wider product card, sketch a **4:3** layout. For a taller poster, sketch a **3:4** layout. Compare the placement of the subject and the empty space for copy in each composition. These ratios are also accepted by the offline image validator.

## Draft a skincare concept prompt

```text
A premium skincare serum bottle with clear glass walls, an ivory inner container, a white pump and brushed gold metal accents. The unbranded bottle floats at a slight diagonal above a glossy black cylindrical pedestal. Suspended water droplets surround it. A charcoal gray studio background, warm rim lighting, soft side light, realistic glass reflections and a subtle pedestal reflection. One main bottle, carefully separated from the background, elegant product photography. No lettering or logos.
```

The prompt describes an imagined product. When reviewing a render, inspect the water droplets, glass reflections and bottle shape for impossible details or duplicates.

For **4:3**, keep the same subject but add: “A wider composition with the bottle slightly right of center and clear negative space on the left.” This leaves room to add accurate copy later.

## Review branding in an editor

Requesting exact brand text can produce misspellings or distorted lettering. For reliable advertising copy, add approved text or a logo in an image editor.

Before publishing, review the pump, cap, bottle outline, shadows and reflections. Check that the visual does not imply an ingredient, certification or product feature that the actual product does not have.

## Animate an existing product photo

1. Open [Image to Video](https://freevideoai.app/ai-video-generator?mode=image-to-video) with a PNG, JPEG or WebP photo you have permission to use, up to 10 MB.
2. For H3 Standard, choose 16:9 or 9:16 and check the framing.
3. Ask for a small camera move and restrained subject motion.
4. Review the quote before submitting. H3 Standard Image to Video costs 8 credits per task.
5. Preview the finished MP4 and inspect the packaging and label before publishing.

Prepare a crop that fits the intended video frame. Read the [product video guide](https://freevideoai.app/product-video-guide) for more examples, and check the [FAQ](https://freevideoai.app/faq) for current availability.
