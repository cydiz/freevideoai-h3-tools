# Create Product-Style Images with H3 Image: 4:3, 3:4 & a Video Follow-Up

H3 Image in FreeVideo AI creates a new image from a text prompt. It is useful for exploring product-advertising concepts, background styling and lighting ideas. The current introductory price is 0 credits, with sign-in, pacing and queue limits. It is not an unlimited batch-generation service.

[Open H3 Image](https://freevideoai.app/ai-image-generator)

## Know what you are creating

The public image tool is **text-to-image**: write a description and generate one image per task. It does not currently offer a product reference upload, image-to-image editing, masks or multiple reference images.

That distinction matters for e-commerce. A generated bottle may look convincing while differing from a real product's cap, proportions or label. Use this workflow for concepts and inspect results carefully. For a listing that must show the exact item, use accurate product photography and an editing workflow that preserves it.

## Choose the right settings

| Setting | Available choices | How to choose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aspect ratio | 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:4 | Match the placement where the image will appear |
| Area level | 1K or 2K | These are area levels, not a fixed long-edge pixel count |
| File format | JPEG, PNG or WebP | Choose the format your destination accepts |
| Prompt | Up to 2,000 characters | Keep the subject and composition clear |

For a product card, try **4:3**. For a taller product poster, try **3:4**. Use 16:9 for a wide hero image or 9:16 for a tall composition. Check the actual pixel dimensions of the result. Selecting PNG does not add a transparent-background feature.

## Generate a luxury skincare concept

1. Sign in to H3 Image.
2. Choose **3:4** for a tall bottle composition.
3. Select an area level and output format.
4. Enter the prompt below.
5. Generate one image and wait for its final status.
6. Inspect the preview, then download the image.

```text
A premium skincare serum bottle with clear glass walls, an ivory inner container, a white pump and brushed gold metal accents. The unbranded bottle floats at a slight diagonal above a glossy black cylindrical pedestal. Suspended water droplets surround it. A charcoal gray studio background, warm rim lighting, soft side light, realistic glass reflections and a subtle pedestal reflection. One main bottle, carefully separated from the background, elegant product photography. No lettering or logos.
```

This is a prompt recipe for an imagined product image, not a promise to reproduce a particular reference photograph. Water droplets, transparent glass and reflections are complex; inspect the output for impossible shapes or duplicated parts.

For **4:3**, keep the same subject but add: “A wider composition with the bottle slightly right of center and clear negative space on the left.” This leaves room to add accurate copy later.

## Add branding after generation

Requesting exact brand text can produce misspellings or distorted lettering. For reliable advertising copy, generate a clean unbranded concept and add approved text or a logo in an image editor. This does not make an invented package an accurate photograph of the real item.

Before publishing, review the pump, cap, bottle outline, shadows and reflections. Check that the visual does not imply an ingredient, certification or product feature that the actual product does not have.

## Turn the downloaded image into a short video

1. Download the image.
2. Open [Image to Video](https://freevideoai.app/ai-video-generator?mode=image-to-video).
3. Upload the downloaded PNG, JPEG or WebP, within the 10 MB video-input limit.
4. Choose the video orientation: **16:9 or 9:16**.
5. Ask for a small camera move and restrained subject motion.
6. Generate and inspect the silent MP4.

This is a manual download-and-upload workflow. There is no public one-click transfer described here. A 3:4 or 4:3 source will not create a 3:4 or 4:3 video output; prepare a matching video composition first if cropping would be a problem. Image to Video costs 8 credits even while the image tool's introductory generation price is 0.

## If a task is waiting

Only one active image task per account is supported. Keep the page or history available and wait for the final status instead of submitting duplicates. Queue capacity and service availability can vary. “Checking the result” means the task may still be reconciled.

Generated history is account-specific; creating an image does not automatically publish it to the site's public examples.
